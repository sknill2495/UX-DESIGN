## 061 · The Dabbawalas: Real Genius, Exaggerated Statistic
**Category:** Business Models · **Subject:** Mumbai Dabbawalas · **Story:** A delivery network over a century old moves lakhs of tiffins a day without smartphones. But the famous "Six Sigma certified by Forbes" claim isn't what it seems.

**Strategy** — Hook: "Six Sigma. Forbes certified. Harvard studied. One of these is not true." · Angle: myth vs reality (flagship Experio episode). · Insight: the system's genius is coding, relay and human ownership, not a magic number. · Duration: 60s · Visuals: Mumbai local trains, coded tiffin lids, a relay map. · Scores: E 10 · S 10 · Sv 8.
**Sources:** [Certain] HBS case: Thomke & Sinha, "The Dabbawala System: On-Time Delivery, Every Time" (2010). [Certain] Wikipedia summary citing the association's own leader saying Forbes never certified them as Six Sigma: https://wikipedia.com/wiki/Dabbawala · [Certain] Varying error-rate claims (one in 6M / 8M / 16M) are summarized here: https://theedgemalaysia.com/article/what-harvard-learning-mumbai-dabbawalas · [Likely] ~5,000 dabbawalas and ~2 lakh tiffins/day is commonly cited. Say "around."

**Script (60s)**
- **0–3 HOOK:** "Dabbawalas: Six Sigma, Forbes certified, Harvard case study. Inmein se ek baat sach nahi hai."
- **3–12 SETUP:** "Mumbai mein 130 saal se zyada se dabbawalas ghar ka khaana office pahuncha rahe hain. Lagbhag 5,000 log, roz lagbhag do lakh dabbe. Na app, na GPS."
- **12–28 STORY:** "System: har dabbe pe ek code, colours, numbers aur symbols. Ghar se pickup, station pe sorting, local train, phir doosre station pe sorting, phir office. Ek dabba kai haathon se guzarta hai, relay race ki tarah. Aur shaam ko khaali dabba wapas."
- **28–45 TWIST:** "Harvard Business School ne sach mein case study likhi. Par 'Forbes ne Six Sigma certify kiya'? Dabbawala association ke hi leader ne kaha hai ki Forbes ne aisa koi certification nahi diya. Error rate ke numbers bhi alag alag jagah alag hain."
