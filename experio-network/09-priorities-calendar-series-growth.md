## 4. Recommendations for growing the brand

**What you probably don't want to hear:**
1. **[Likely] Faceless motion-graphics will lose to a host on camera at this budget.** The Hinglish explainer channels in §2 that win all have a trusted face. If the founder won't be on camera, hire a host before buying an animation package.
2. **[Certain] 100 ideas is a backlog, not a strategy.** The strategy is the 12 series. Retire any series that underperforms the account median on sends per reach for 3 consecutive episodes, even if you love it.
3. **[Certain] One factual error in a "fact-checking" brand costs more than ten good videos earn.** Run a two-person source check on every Myth vs Mechanism and AI episode. Re-verify anything tagged [Verify] or [Likely] the week you record.

**Operating system:**
- **Batch production:** record 8 scripts per session (two weeks of content). Edit 4 per editing day.
- **Template every edit:** hook text (frame 1), source card (once, 2s), series tag, caption style, end card. That keeps the look consistent and saves editing time.
- **Metrics that matter (weekly review):** Instagram sends per reach, saves per reach, 3-second hold / average watch %, and YouTube viewed-vs-swiped-away. Treat likes and follower count as secondary.
- **Comment-to-content loop:** every Sunday, pull the 5 best questions from comments. They become next month's ideas, and the commenter gets credited in the video, which gives people a reason to comment.
- **Pinned comment = source + question.** It builds trust and starts the comment thread.
- **Collaborations:** Instagram Collab posts with Indian UX/design and finance creators on the Dark Pattern Detective series. Viewers are already used to that kind of crossover.
- **Owned channel:** a weekly email ("The Experio Letter: 3 experiences explained") from month 2, so that the audience isn't 100% dependent on the platforms.
- **Long-form YouTube:** one compilation per month (see calendar). Shorts feed discovery, and long-form builds the people who stay subscribed.
- **Legal hygiene:** never re-upload ads or film clips. Use stock (Pexels/Pixabay/licensed), your own footage, screen recordings of public pages, and recreations. When referencing brand logos, show them in an editorial context only.

**90-day success definition:** track the sends and saves rate trend and series recognition (comments that name a series), not raw follower count. If by week 13 viewers are commenting things like "Myth vs Mechanism pe X karo," the brand is working.
